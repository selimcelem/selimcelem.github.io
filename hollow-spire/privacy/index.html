Hollow Spire

Privacy Policy · effective 8 September 2026

Your climb stays on your phone. Hollow Spire has no accounts, advertising or analytics. Gameplay works offline. From version 100.7, the app uses Google's Play In-App Updates library to check for updates and offer an optional download through Google Play.

What the game stores

Your progress: the Manor, the vault, the line of heirs and the climb in progress. It is written to the app's own private storage on your device. Nothing in it identifies you, and none of it leaves the phone through the game.

Uninstalling Hollow Spire deletes it. Erasing a line inside the game deletes that line.

What the game does not do

  • It asks for no Android permissions.
  • It sends no saved progress to the developer or to Google Play's update service.
  • It shows no ads and contains no purchases.
  • It has no login, no account and no cloud save of its own.
  • It includes no analytics, crash reporting or tracking libraries.

Android and Google Play

For update checks, Google Play processes device metadata, the app version and the list of installed modules and asset packs to determine availability and download size. Google documents that this data is encrypted, is not transferred to third parties and is deleted after a fixed retention period. See Google's in-app update data information.

You can decline an update and keep playing. Downloads use Google Play; the game asks before restarting to install an update. Your save is kept in the app's existing storage.

If device backup is switched on in your Android settings, Android may include the game's save in the backup it keeps for your Google account. That is an Android feature under Google's control and Google's privacy policy, and you can turn it off in Android settings.

Google Play may collect install, update and crash statistics about apps you use. That happens in Google Play, not in the game, and is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Children

Hollow Spire is made for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. There is no game sign-in or messaging. Your progress is saved on your own device; Google Play's handling of update information is described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at this address with a new effective date.

A later version of Hollow Spire may offer an optional Google Play Games sign-in so your climb can be saved online as well as on the phone. Nothing about that is in the game today. Before a version with it ships, this policy will say exactly what that sign-in shares and how to stay offline.
